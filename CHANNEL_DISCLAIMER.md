## 8. YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box: when to tick it

Fully enforced since January 2026. It is required for **realistic** content a viewer could mistake for a real person, place or event
(searched 2026-10-01; secondary sources, so check YouTube Help before relying on the edge cases).

| This channel's content | Tick the box? |
|---|---|
| Stock AI narrator (e.g. ElevenLabs "Brian") over charts | Not required. A synthetic voice that doesn't imitate a real person doesn't trigger it |
| **Your own cloned voice** ("Sheldon - Voice") | **Tick it.** Clones of a real person's voice are named in the policy, even your own |
| Realistic AI B-roll that reads as real footage (city, trading floor) | **Tick it.** It could be mistaken for a real place |
| AI footage of a real product doing something it hasn't done (the Roadster thruster clip) | **Tick it, and keep the on-screen "AI ILLUSTRATION" label** |
| Charts, motion graphics, AI-assisted scripts or thumbnails | Not required |

Default: **when in doubt, tick it.** Disclosing costs nothing. Not disclosing can mean a forced label or penalties.
