# The Meticulous Investor — channel disclaimer

Copy-paste text for YouTube. Consistent with `COMPLIANCE.md` §8, which still governs the
per-video on-screen cards and the description footer.

## 1. Channel "About" section (YouTube limit: 1,000 characters)

```
Real numbers, sourced facts, and the data that cuts against the story.

DISCLAIMER: All content on this channel is for educational and informational purposes only. It is market commentary, not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Past performance does not indicate future results, and all investing involves risk, including loss of principal. Figures are accurate as of the dates shown and may have changed. Hypothetical examples are for illustration only. Forecasts belong to the analysts or institutions named, not this channel. The creator is not a licensed financial adviser. Do your own research and consider speaking with a licensed professional before making any investment decision.

Narration and visuals are produced with the help of AI tools. Every figure is researched and checked before publishing.
```

## 2. Pinned comment (short, for every video)

```
Educational only, not financial advice. Figures are as of the dates shown; sources and full disclaimer in the description.
```

## 3. Holdings disclosure — pick ONE and add it to the About text and each description

Only the channel owner knows which is true, so this line is deliberately left as a choice:

```
The creator holds no positions in the securities discussed in this video.
```
```
The creator may hold positions in the securities discussed. This is disclosed so you can weigh any bias.
```

## 4. Only if you ever add affiliate or sponsored links

```
Some links may be affiliate links; the channel may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsorships are always disclosed in the video.
```
When a video is sponsored, also tick **"Video contains paid promotion"** in YouTube Studio.

## Notes

- **AI disclosure.** YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" checkbox is required for *realistic*
  content that could be mistaken for real people or events — an AI voice narrating charts
  generally does not trigger it. The About line above covers transparency either way. Tick the box
  if a video ever shows a realistic synthetic person, place or event.
- **Per-video text** stays as locked in `COMPLIANCE.md` §8: opening card in the first 4 seconds,
  closing card on the final frame, description footer verbatim.
- This is standard industry wording, not legal advice. If the channel grows, sells a course,
  or takes sponsorships, have a lawyer review it once.
