## Counter-evidence

The video does not say sweeps are bad. **Spread across enough partner banks, each tranche
is covered** — that is the mechanism working as designed. The failure mode is specific:
cash concentrated at one program bank, and the assumption that SIPC backstops a market
fall. The close is a check, not an instruction: open the list of banks in your account.
