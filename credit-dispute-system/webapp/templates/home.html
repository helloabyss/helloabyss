Find errors in your credit reports and write accurate dispute letters

Enter what your Equifax, Experian and TransUnion reports show, upload your documents, and tell us what's wrong. The app checks each account against the Fair Credit Reporting Act and related laws, then prepares dispute letters for you to sign and mail yourself.

What it does

  • Flags accounts reported differently across bureaus, debts past the 7-year limit, re-aged collections, paid accounts still showing balances, and more.
  • Builds identity-theft block requests when you have an FTC Identity Theft Report.
  • Cites the specific law for every item and tracks the bureaus' legal deadlines.

What it doesn't do

  • It won't dispute information you tell us is accurate. Nobody can lawfully remove accurate, current information.
  • It doesn't promise deletions or a higher score.
  • It isn't a law firm and doesn't give legal advice.
