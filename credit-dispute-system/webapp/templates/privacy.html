Privacy policy

{{ company.name }} · {{ company.address }}

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password.
  • Your case: name, other names you've used, date of birth, last 4 digits of your Social Security number, current and past addresses, phone (optional), and the credit report details and statements you enter.
  • Documents you upload: ID, proof of address and supporting documents.
  • Records the law requires: your signed acknowledgment of the "Consumer Credit File Rights" statement and your agreement, with the date, time and IP address of signing.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

Only to run your audit, prepare your letters, send you account and deadline emails, and meet our legal record-keeping duties. We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you. We do not contact credit bureaus or creditors for you.

How we protect it

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Case data and documents are encrypted in storage. Only you, and staff of a firm that opened your case for you, can see your case.

Service providers

Hosting (Render), payments (Stripe) and email delivery process data for us under their own security and privacy terms.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete a case, or your whole account and all its data, at any time from My cases. Deletion is immediate; encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days. The Credit Repair Organizations Act requires us to keep your signed rights acknowledgment for 2 years, so that one record (your name, email and signing date) is kept for 2 years and then deleted automatically.

Phone apps

The apps use your camera only when you choose to photograph a document, and send deadline reminders as notifications on your phone. Reminder text never names a creditor or bureau.
