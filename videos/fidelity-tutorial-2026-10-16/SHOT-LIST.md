# Shot list — Fidelity tutorial

You record; I edit. Free — no credits used anywhere.

## Before you hit record

- **Computer, not phone.** Browser full-screen, window 1920×1080 if you can, zoom 125%
  so text is readable on a phone.
- **Recorder:** Mac — `Cmd+Shift+5`, tick "Microphone" in Options. Windows — `Win+Alt+R`
  (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS (free).
- **Quiet room, mic close.** Headset or AirPods is fine.
- Close other tabs, hide the bookmarks bar, turn notifications off.
- **Log in first, then start recording.** Never record the login page or a 2-step code.
- **Never click "Place order."** Every trade ticket stops at **Preview**.
- Don't worry about your numbers — I blur account numbers, names, balances and the
  Fidelity logo in the edit. If you'd rather, record in an account with a small balance.

## How to record

- **One file per shot** (below). Name them `shot1`, `shot2`… if you can.
- Read the matching chapter of `SCRIPT.md` **while you click**. Your own words are fine.
- **Move the mouse slowly** and **rest it 2 seconds** on each thing you name — I add the
  zooms and red highlights on those pauses.
- **Messed up a line?** Stop, wait 3 seconds, say the sentence again. I cut the bad take.
- Upload the files here. They stay out of the public GitHub repo.

## If your screen doesn't match

Fidelity changes its layout. If a tab or button has a different name, use the name you
see and say it out loud — I'll match the captions to what you say.
