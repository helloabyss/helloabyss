Ninety-three percent.
That's how many large-cap fund managers lost to the plain S&P 500.
Over twenty years.
Not financial advice, so do your own research.
These are pros. Research teams. Data terminals. Big salaries.
And in 2025 alone, seventy-nine percent of them still trailed the index.
So why not just pick the winners?
Because the winners don't stay winners.
Take the top-quarter funds of 2021.
By the end of 2025, less than half a percent were still there.
Fewer than one in two hundred.
To be fair, active funds had a better start to 2026.
Only sixty-seven percent lagged in the first half.
Still two in three.
And an index fund won't save you in a crash. It falls with the market.
What it does is take the fees out of the fight.
Tonight, look up one number on every fund you own: the expense ratio.
It's the one part of your return you know in advance.
