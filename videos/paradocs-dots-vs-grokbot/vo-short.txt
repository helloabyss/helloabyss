OpenAI just gave you one AI agent. Elon Musk's xAI gives you a whole team.

OpenAI's Dots launched September twenty-ninth. Each dot gets its own cloud computer, connects to over four thousand apps, and keeps working while you're away.

xAI's Grok Bot came first. You run several bots on one shared machine, and they message each other in a group chat to split the work.

The catch? A dot needs ChatGPT Pro, from a hundred dollars a month, and Pro users in the UK and Europe can't get one yet. Grok Bot starts at twenty dollars, but every bot shares the same logins.

One careful agent, or a cheap crew? Comment which one you'd hire.
