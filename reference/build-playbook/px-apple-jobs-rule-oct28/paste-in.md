## Title
Jobs Said Touchscreen Laptops "Don't Work." Apple May Try on Oct 28

## Description
In 2010 Steve Jobs said touch on a vertical laptop screen "doesn't work." Apple's "Pro and tell" event is Wednesday, Oct 28, 2026, at 10 AM PT (10 AM Arizona) in Los Angeles, and Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports a touchscreen MacBook Pro with an OLED display, in 14-inch and 16-inch sizes. Apple has not announced it.

What's in the video: Jobs on gorilla arm (Oct 2010), Phil Schiller's 2016 "absurd" comment on touch Macs, the reported reason Apple is changing course (Tim Cook and John Ternus say customers expect touch; designers pushed back), the reported "significantly higher" price, and the iPhone Duo's letterboxed apps at launch.
