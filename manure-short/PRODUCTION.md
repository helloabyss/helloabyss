## Prompt guardrails specific to this one
- **No real newspaper masthead.** The script says The Times denied the attribution, so
  rendering a real Times masthead would put a real publication's brand on a claim about it.
  Newspapers are generic and unbranded.
- **Archival and cold, not warm sepia.** Period imagery defaults to nostalgic; the tone
  needed is investigative.
- **Cross-dissolve from the identical camera position** on the 1912 avenue shot, so the
  horse-to-motorcar switch reads as change in place rather than as two unrelated plates.
- Figures in period street scenes must be distant silhouettes only (faceless rule).

## Accuracy note — do not loosen in a re-cut
The 1898 international urban planning conference **did happen**, and manure dominated it.
What traces to the 2004 essay is the dramatised version: the Times quote, the fifty-year
figure, and the conference collapsing in despair. The VO is worded precisely for this.
Claiming "the conference never happened" would be false.
