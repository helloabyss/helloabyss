## 2. Scripts

Each Short: **"Educational only. Not financial advice."** in the first 4 seconds
*and* on the last frame.

### Short 1 — TWO DATES
> October 1 is a Roadster reveal. October 6 is the FSD Europe vote. Different machines. Don't trade them as one.

### Short 2 — REVEAL ≠ REVENUE
> Nearly nine years since the 2017 prototype. Musk's own formula: production 12 to 18 months after the demo. Not this quarter's cash.

*(Revision: "Nine years" → "Nearly nine years." Nov 2017 to Oct 2026 is 8 years 10.5 months.)*

### Short 3 — 2017 SPECS ARE STILL CLAIMS
> 1.9 seconds, 250-plus, 620 miles are still on Tesla.com. That is a claim sheet, not a tested production car.

### Short 4 — DON'T PAY TWICE
> If the multiple already includes a halo car and Europe FSD, a pretty stage does not add a new story.

On screen: `FRAMING, NOT A PRICE CALL` — no multiple, no target, no chart.

### Short 5 — WHICH ROW DO YOU OWN?
> Country headline. October 6 vote. Unsupervised robotaxi. Roadster deliveries. Pick one row. Not financial advice.

Four-row table builds; each row gets a state stamp: `HAPPENED` / `PENDING` / `NOT YET` / `2027–28`.

### Short 6 — SWEDEN SAYS NO *(added)*
**On-screen:** `ONE STATE ALREADY SAID NO`
**VO (~22s):**
> Sweden has told the committee to vote against, unless Tesla drops the setting that lets the car exceed the limit. Seven approvals is the number people quote. A committed no is the number they don't.

This is the counter-evidence beat the guide requires, and it's the fact that makes the
pack an explainer rather than a countdown. Cut Short 4 for it if the pack must stay at five.
