## 2. Claims in the source clip — checked

The mechanism the clip teaches is **sound**. Two of its historical anchors are **wrong**, and
one is unverifiable. Per `CLAUDE.md` ("include the disconfirming data"), the correction is in
the episode — as a correction of a widely repeated claim, not as an attack on the creator.

| Clip claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "The ten-year note is now at 5 percent… it's at 5.1 percent" | ✅ **Correct** | Fact 1 |
| 10-year is a risk-free rate that competes with equities for capital | ✅ **Correct** | Standard valuation mechanics; conceptually sound as stated |
| Higher yields hit growth/AI/software hardest | ✅ **Correct** | Duration logic; converges with reporting on the day's move |
| AI infrastructure (data centres, chips, cooling, networking) is debt-financed, so 5% raises the hurdle | ✅ **Correct** | Directionally sound and widely reported |
| 10-year rises → mortgage rates rise → hits buyers, builders, banks, consumer spending | ✅ **Correct, and confirmed live** | Fact 6: mortgage hit 7.03% the same week |
| **"In August of 2024… the ten-year had reached 5 percent"** | ❌ **False — and backwards** | The 10-year **fell** from **4.28% to 3.73%** between 2024-07-24 and 2024-08-05, a 55bp *drop*. The August 2024 selloff was the yen carry-trade unwind after the BoJ hiked to ~0.25% on 2024-07-31; the Nikkei fell 12.4% on Aug 5. Yields fell because money ran *into* Treasuries |
| **"Biden came on TV and said hey, hold up"** because of it | ❌ **Unverifiable** | No support found. Conflated with the same month's recession scare |
| **April 2025 tariff pause happened "because the ten-year had hit 5 percent"** | ⚠️ **Half right** | The *direction* is right — Fortune and others report bond yields, not equities, were what moved Trump. But the **level was ~4.5%, not 5%**: the 10-year fell to 3.86% on Apr 4 then surged past 4.5% by Apr 11. It was the **speed** of the move that forced the pause, not a 5% print |
| "Donald Trump definitely going to have a lot to say about this today" | — | Speculation, not a claim. Not carried into the script |
