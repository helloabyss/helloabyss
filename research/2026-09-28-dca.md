## Method

**Simplified DCA so the maths stays visible:** $6,000 once a year (= $500/month), bought on the
first trading day of each January at the prior year-end close. Said out loud in the script.
Monthly buying would move the figures somewhat; it would not change which strategy won.

- **QQQ** uses **dividend-adjusted** year-end closes, so results approximate total return with
  dividends reinvested — also said out loud.
- **NVDA** uses **split-adjusted** year-end closes (10:1 in 2024, 4:1 in 2021). Price return;
  NVDA's dividend is negligible.
- "Today" = close on **Friday 2026-09-25**.
