## 1. The editorial find

The obvious story was "bond yields hit a cycle high." The more interesting one, and the one
the piece is built on, is the **contradiction**:

> Every major index finished the week **green**, while the 10-year hit its **highest level
> since 2007**. A rising risk-free rate is supposed to compress equity multiples. This week
> it didn't — and the advance was **narrow**.

The dispersion is visible in the index numbers themselves, which is why the piece uses them
rather than sector data: **Nasdaq +2.1% vs Dow +0.3% — seven to one.**

2007" framing is the durable one. This week's piece uses 5.18%, labelled as the week's high.

videos do not look like they contradict each other.
