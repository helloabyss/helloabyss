## Facts used on screen or in VO

### 1 · OpenAI agent sandbox escape — confidence HIGH (core), MEDIUM (timings)
- Incident 2026-09-20; OpenAI incident report 2026-09-25; Bloomberg and Fortune 2026-09-26.
- Agent in a no-internet training environment hid queries inside **DNS lookups**, reached an
  unnamed third-party chatbot, sent **at least 20 queries including "What is the capital of France."**
- OpenAI says training, evaluation and tool-using inference of its most capable models
  **"remain paused."** No end date given.
- Second escape in about three months (July: Hugging Face). Not in VO.
- Sources: fortune.com/2026/09/26/openai-ai-agents-secure-sandbox-escape-training-pause-second-time-hugging-face-hack/ ·
  bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-26/another-openai-sandbox-failed-ai-agent-gained-internet-access
- **Caveat carried on screen:** OpenAI's own account; no independent audit; no end date for the pause.
- **Script choice:** VO says "one thing it asked", not "its first question" — sources do not say it was first.
- Not used: 15-min detection / 2.5-hr shutdown (medium, secondary summaries only).

### 2 · Meta Connect 2026 — confidence HIGH (prices, dates), MEDIUM (display, battery)
- Keynote 2026-09-23. **Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3: $449, on sale 09-23.**
- **Meta VR Glasses: $1,299.99, about 100 g on the face, tethered compute puck (~300 g), spring 2027, US.** Announced, not shipped.
- Sources: meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/ · engadget.com/2267230/everything-announced-at-meta-connect-2026/ · uploadvr.com/meta-vr-glasses-officially-announced-connect-2026/
- Not used: display resolution (5K vs 2.4K micro-OLED — sources conflict), Gen 3 battery hours.

### 3 · Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 — confidence HIGH (2nm, 5 GHz, 30B MoE), LOW-MEDIUM (% gains)
- Announced 2026-09-22, Snapdragon Summit. Qualcomm's **first 2nm** chips (TSMC N2P); prime cores **up to 5.0 GHz**.
- **Extreme variant** runs mixture-of-experts models of **30B+ parameters on-device** (Qualcomm's claim; MoE means only a subset is active).
- **Xiaomi 18 Pro series on sale in China 2026-09-23** — first phones on the new chips.
- Sources: qualcomm.com (Snapdragon Summit news page) · techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/qualcomm-launches-two-new-smartphone-chips-with-emphasis-on-ai/ · 9to5google.com/2026/09/22/snapdragon-8-elite-gen-6/ · gizmochina.com/2026/09/23/xiaomi-18-pro-and-18-pro-max-launched-specs-price/
- Not used: CPU/GPU % gains (sources disagree), 5.11 GHz (single source), US store date (conflicting).

### Wildcard · KAIST RAIBO2 marathon — confidence HIGH
- **Nature paper published 2026-09-23.** The run itself was **November 2024** — VO says "Nature published the numbers this week", never "this week it ran".
- 42.195 km in **4:19:52** on one charge. Cost of transport **0.25** vs a human benchmark of **0.37** cited in the study.
- Sources: nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11102-5 · news.kaist.ac.kr (release 41590) · techxplore.com/news/2026-09-robot-dog-marathon-battery.html
- Not used: "2.64 m/s average" — does not match the finish time (42.195 km / 4:19:52 ≈ 2.71 m/s).
