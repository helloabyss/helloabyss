mechanism, adds the live confirmation the clip didn't have (the mortgage print landed the

data, and because a viewer who repeats the August-2024 claim will be wrong in public.

The correction is framed against **"the version going around"**, not against the creator.
No name, no channel, no screenshot, no dunk. The point is the fact, not the person.
