## Clips — monetisation-safe sourcing

### Safe
1. **Charts you build in Agent Opus from the numbers above.** Original work, can't be
   Content-ID'd, and the numbers are the point of the video. Aim for over half the runtime.
2. **Agent Opus's own generated visuals.** Confirm your plan's licence covers commercial use.
3. **Free stock, commercial use allowed:** Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr. Download only from
   the official site and **save the clip's page URL**. Free clips are sometimes wrongly
   registered in Content ID by other uploaders; the URL is your proof when you dispute a claim.
   Claims affect revenue on that video; they are not copyright strikes.
4. **Paid stock:** Storyblocks, Envato Elements, Artgrid, Adobe Stock. Keep the licence
   certificate for each clip.
5. **Music:** YouTube Audio Library is the safest option, because YouTube's own library won't
   trigger claims. If you use Epidemic Sound or Artlist, whitelist your channel on their site
   first.

### Never use — these are the usual causes of claims, strikes or demonetisation on finance channels
- **Financial TV:** CNBC, Bloomberg, Fox Business, Yahoo Finance — even 5 seconds.
- **Nvidia keynotes / Jensen Huang / Nvidia product videos** — Nvidia's copyright, and a face.
- **Movie & TV clips:** *The Wolf of Wall Street*, *The Big Short*, *Margin Call*, *Billions*.
- **Broker app screen recordings** (Robinhood, etc.) — trademarks, and against the channel's no-logo rule.
- **Nvidia, Invesco or Nasdaq logos** — write the names as plain text instead.
- **Any commercial song**, however short.
- **Other creators' charts, thumbnails or footage.**
- **Faces** — the channel is faceless; use back-of-head, out-of-focus or hands-only shots.

### YouTube policy notes
- **Inauthentic / reused content:** an original script, original narration and your own charts
  count as transformative. What gets flagged is stock footage cut together with little or no
  original commentary.
- **Advertiser-friendly:** no "get rich" promises. The script frames results as history and the
  forward numbers as hypothetical, with a disclaimer at both ends. Keep it that way in the title
  and thumbnail too.
