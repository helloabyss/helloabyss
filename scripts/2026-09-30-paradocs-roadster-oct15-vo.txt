Tesla's Roadster just got another date. October 15th.

The reveal was set for October 1st, outdoors near Waco, Texas. The forecast showed an eighty-two percent chance of thunderstorms. So Tesla moved it.

Weather is a fair reason. The pattern is the story.

The Roadster was unveiled in 2017, with production promised for 2020. Since then, the date has moved at least a dozen times.

A reveal is still not a delivery. This is Paradocs 10X.
