## ⚠️ CORRECTIONS AND CAUTIONS — act on these before publishing

1. **"today, 21 September" is now WRONG.** It is **22 September**. Shorts 1 and 2 both say
   "today". Change to "yesterday" or just "on 21 September". *This alone would have dated
   the video incorrectly on upload.*

2. **The legal citation is shakier than the brief implies.** The brief cites
   *Regulation (EU) 2018/858 Art. 39*. Sources describe the technical basis as
   **UN Regulation 171 (DCAS)**, with RDW calling it a "European type approval with
   provisional validity in the Netherlands". Art. 39 is the plausible *procedural* route
   (new-technology exemption, validity limited to the granting state) and UN R171 the
   *technical* regulation — but no source I reached states "Art. 39" explicitly.
   **Recommend: say "the EU's provisional approval route for new technology" on screen and
   drop the article number**, or verify it against the regulation text first.

3. **Do not use the Short 5 quote verbatim.** The brief quotes Tesla Europe as
   *"FSD Supervised now approved in Czechia. Rollout will begin soon."* I could **not**
   verify that exact wording. A fabricated quote attributed to a company is a serious
   problem. **Paraphrase** ("Tesla says rollout begins soon") unless you have the post open.

4. **The ~11.8% figure is sound, but it is *computed*, not sourced.** Summing the seven
   countries (≈53.0M) against EU-27 (≈449M) gives **11.8%** — so the number is right, but
   it is arithmetic on population data, not a figure any source published. Label it as such.
   The gap to the 65% threshold is roughly **240M people** — a useful, honest framing.

5. **Per-country approval dates are tracker-sourced, not primary.** The brief's dates for
   Lithuania (20 May), Estonia (29 May), Denmark (9 June), Belgium (10 June), Slovenia
   (7 Sept) come from trackers. The *order* and the *set* are well corroborated; the exact
   days are not. The Shorts don't state them (fine). **The 8-min outline's "simple dated
   list" does** — either verify each or show the sequence without dates.

6. **"Yield products can lose NAV" does not belong here.** It appears in the pinned comment
   and SKIP list, but nothing in this piece concerns yield or income products. It is
   boilerplate from a different lane and will read as careless. **Remove it.** The two
   disclaimers that *do* apply are "Not financial advice" and "FSD Supervised is supervised".

7. **Style conflict, flagged not resolved.** The brief specifies **dark navy**; the channel's
   locked `STYLE-GUIDE.md` is near-black / white / one red. Built to the brief's navy here
   since it was specified explicitly — but it will not match the other videos.

## Strengthening detail the brief omits
Czechia had **publicly raised safety concerns first** — how FSD Supervised handles traffic
lights and speed limits, and how it confirms the driver is still paying attention — and
approved only after additional European data and monitoring safeguards. That is the
counter-evidence beat `STYLE-GUIDE.md` requires, and it makes the piece stronger, not weaker.
