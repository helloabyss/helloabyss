The Fed held rates yesterday. Three point seven five to four percent.

The bigger change was the calendar.

Eight meetings in twenty twenty-seven. Only seven will set rates.

One is strategy only. No vote. No statement.

Twenty twenty-eight drops to six rate decisions.

Fewer scheduled moments to respond, and more weight on each one left.

But it is not a cap. The Fed can move between meetings, and has.

Eight was convention, never law.

Before you mark a twenty twenty-seven date as a decision, check which kind it is.
