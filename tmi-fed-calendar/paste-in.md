## Title
The Fed Just Removed a 2027 Rate Decision. Eight Meetings, Seven Votes.

## Description
```
The Fed held rates at 3.75%-4% on 28 October 2026, in a 10-2 vote. Two officials
dissented in favour of a quarter-point HIKE, which is the unusual part - a sitting
governor dissenting on a rate vote is rare.

But the bigger change came in the press conference. From 2027 the FOMC calendar
changes shape:

  2027   eight meetings, but only SEVEN set rates
         one is strategy-only: no rate vote, no policy statement
  2028   two strategy meetings, six rate decisions

Fewer scheduled moments to respond, and more weight on each one that is left.

What this is NOT: a cap on action. The FOMC can move between scheduled meetings and
has done so before. Eight meetings a year is the Committee's own convention, not a
statutory requirement - so removing one is a change of habit, not a loss of power.
And a strategy meeting still happens; it just carries no rate vote.

Figures are corroborated secondary reporting (CNBC, CNN, Yahoo Finance). The Federal
Reserve's own pages were not reachable at the time of writing, so nothing here is
confirmed against the primary release.

Before you mark a 2027 date as a decision, check which kind of meeting it is.

Drop a comment.

```
