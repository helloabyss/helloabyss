## Grade

reporting corroborated across at least two independent outlets. One source
(investinglive) carried garbled statement text and was discarded.

## Counter-evidence — must be in the script
1. **It is not a cap on action.** The FOMC can and has moved between scheduled
   meetings. Fewer scheduled dates does not mean fewer possible moves.
2. **Eight was convention, not law.** The number of meetings per year is the
   Committee's own practice, not a statutory requirement, so "removing" one is
   a change of habit rather than a loss of power.
3. **A strategy meeting is not a cancelled meeting.** It still happens; it just
   carries no rate vote and no statement.
