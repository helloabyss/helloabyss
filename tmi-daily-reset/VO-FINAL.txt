The index is flat.

The three-times fund, down one point six percent.

Index down five. Fund down fifteen.

Day two, straight back.

From ninety-five: plus five point two six.

Triple it. Fifteen point eight.

But it compounds from eighty-five.

Ninety-eight point four two.

Twenty repeats: still flat, down twenty-seven.

Three times the daily move. Never the return.

Now the other side.

Trend two days and it pays you.

Three up days beat three times the index.

That is what these are for.

Decay is a path, not a law.

Two days, multiplied, not added. That gap is the reset.
