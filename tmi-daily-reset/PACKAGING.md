## Description

```
A 3x fund multiplies the index's DAILY move, not its return. It rebalances every
evening, so two offsetting days do not cancel out.

Worked example, illustrative, not any real fund:

  Day 1   index -5%      fund -15%     100 -> 85
  Day 2   index +5.26%   fund +15.79%  85 -> 98.42

The index is exactly flat. The fund is down 1.58%. Repeat that swing twenty times and
the index is still flat while the fund is down 27.3%.

The other side, which matters just as much: this cuts both ways. In a steady trend the
same daily reset compounds in your favour. Three up days of +5% give the index +15.76%,
so 3x that is +47.29% - but the fund returns +52.09%. It beats three times the index.
That is what these products are built for. Decay is path-dependent, not a law.

Every figure here is arithmetic you can reproduce on a calculator. No fund is named, no
price is quoted, and no tracking history is claimed.
```
