## How to use this guide

Most "GTA 6 guides" for sale right now were written before anyone played the game. They
pad the page count with rumours presented as facts. This guide does the opposite. Each
claim is labelled:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CONFIRMED** | Stated by Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft. |
| **REPORTED** | Several credible outlets report it, but there is no first-party statement. |
| **RUMOUR** | Speculation. Listed only so you can ignore it on purpose. |

If Rockstar changes something, this guide gets updated and you get the new edition free.

## Part 2 — The storage plan (do this tonight)

The game needs up to 212 GB **free**, and patches arrive on top of that. Plan for
**250 GB free** so a launch patch doesn't block you.

### Find out where you stand

| Console | Usable storage out of the box (approx.) | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 (825 GB model) | ~667 GB | Usually fine. Check the free space you actually have. |
| PS5 Slim / Pro (1 TB / 2 TB) | ~848 GB / ~1.8 TB | Usually fine. |
| Xbox Series X (1 TB) | ~802 GB | Usually fine. |
| **Xbox Series S (512 GB)** | **~362 GB** | **High risk.** The game takes more than half the drive. Clear space first. |

*Usable-space figures are approximate system numbers and vary with firmware.*

### The 10-minute clear-out
1. **Sort installed games by size** in your console's storage menu.
2. **Uninstall anything you haven't played in 60 days.** Your saves stay in the cloud.
   Check this first if you don't have PS Plus or Xbox cloud saves turned on.
3. **Delete old video captures.** Captures can take up tens of gigabytes without you
   noticing.
4. **Move games to external storage.** On both consoles, a USB drive can *store* current-gen
   games, but they **must run from internal storage or an approved expansion**. Use the USB
   drive as a parking spot, not a place to play from.
5. **Check again:** you're aiming for 250 GB free.

### Pre-load checklist
- [ ] The pre-order is on the **same account** you'll play on
- [ ] Auto-updates are on, so the day-one patch installs overnight
- [ ] The console is in rest mode (PS5) or "Sleep" power mode (Xbox), so it can download
  while it's off
- [ ] Check on the morning of the 18th that the download says **100%**, not "waiting"

## Part 5 — Your first session: habits that pay off in any Rockstar open world

None of this depends on unreleased mechanics. These are habits that have worked across
Rockstar's open-world games, and they'll be checked against the real game in Edition 1.1.

1. **Go through every settings menu before the first mission.** Subtitles, camera
   sensitivity, aim assist, HUD size and audio mix. Changing them 10 hours in feels worse
   than spending 10 minutes now.
2. **Turn on any performance or quality mode you prefer** (if the game offers one) before
   you form an opinion of how it looks.
3. **Save by hand before anything big.** Don't rely only on autosaves.
4. **Do the story first and explore after.** Rockstar often unlocks activities and map
   areas through story progress. Exploring too early can mean finding locked doors.
5. **Don't spend all your in-game money early.** Rockstar economies usually reward patience,
   such as property or business purchases unlocked later. *(We'll check whether this holds
   in VI.)*
6. **Write down where things are.** A plain notes app beats trying to remember a shop, a
   hidden spot or a side-activity location three days later.
7. **Take a break every 2 hours.** Launch-week burnout is real.

## Part 6 — Spoiler lockdown

Leaks and early copies tend to hit social media before launch.

- **YouTube:** pause watch history, and click "Not interested" on any GTA VI upload titled
  "ending", "full story" or "all missions".
- **X / Threads / Bluesky:** mute these words: `GTA 6 ending`, `GTA VI ending`, `Lucia dies`,
  `Jason dies`, `final mission`, `leaked gameplay`.
- **Reddit:** stay out of general gaming subreddits until you've finished the story.
- **TikTok:** long-press and choose "Not interested" on the first spoiler you see. The
  algorithm learns quickly.

*The muted-word list above is a filter list, not a hint about the plot. Nobody outside
Rockstar knows the ending yet.*

## Part 7 — For creators: record your launch properly

If you plan to post clips, set this up **before** launch night.

- **Read Rockstar's video policy** (Rockstar Support: *"Policy on posting copyrighted
  Rockstar Games material"*). Gameplay footage is generally allowed. **Using Rockstar logos
  or official art to suggest you're endorsed is not.**
- **Licensed in-game radio music can trigger Content ID claims.** Turn the radio down or
  off for clips you plan to post.
- **PS5:** set the capture length and resolution in *Settings › Captures and Broadcasts*.
- **Xbox:** set the clip length and resolution in *Settings › Preferences › Capture & share*.
- **Mark ending and late-story uploads as spoilers** in the title and thumbnail. Audiences
  remember who spoiled them.
