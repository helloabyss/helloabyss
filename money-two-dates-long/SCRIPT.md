## 4. Changes from the outline

1. **5:00 beat added.** `STYLE-GUIDE.md` §5 makes the counter-evidence beat mandatory, and
   the outline had none: it argued the two dates shouldn't be stacked, but never gave the
   reasons the Oct 6 vote could fail. Sweden's committed no, the sealed safety file, and
   the self-measured 4.1× are the honest counterweight. Adds ~40s.
2. **4:20 beat** gains the France comparison (~15.3% vs ~11.8%) — the outline had it in the
   FSD-only script only, and it is the clearest way to show the gap is not nearly closed.
3. **8:00 close.** Outline said "Subscribe." `CLAUDE.md` bans subscribe CTAs and
   `STYLE-GUIDE.md` §5 wants an actionable close. Replaced with the next dated thing.
4. **0:00 compliance card** added (first 4 seconds, house rule).
5. **$TSLA** appears as plain text. No candles, no chart, no price.
6. **Waco vs McGregor** — the invite and the flight restriction name different towns. Say
   "Waco" as the invite does; do not put a map on screen implying one site.
