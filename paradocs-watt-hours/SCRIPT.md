# PARADOCS10X — "0.24 Wh. Or 0.10."

**38.48s · 16 VO beats → 29 shots · 1080×1920 · 30fps · Alex Wright · no host**

## The claim

Google's own paper gives two energy figures for the **same median Gemini Apps text
prompt**, and both are correct. The number moves because the measurement boundary moves.

| Figure | What it counts | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **0.10 Wh** | Active TPU/GPU only — the chip doing the inference | **Medium-high** |
| **0.17 Wh** | + host machine, idle capacity, data-centre overhead | **Medium** |
| **0.24 Wh** | + averaged across the whole fleet. Google's headline comprehensive figure | **Medium-high** |
| **2.4× spread** | 0.24 ÷ 0.10, arithmetic | **High** |
| Based on **May 2025** data | Stated in the paper | **Medium-high** |

**Nothing here is CONFIRMED.** The paper itself and Google's own blog were not opened —
every primary source is DNS-blocked from this environment. These are corroborated
secondary reports, consistent across independent outlets.

## Counter-evidence — 7 of 16 beats

The video explicitly says **nothing was hidden**: Google published all three numbers and
the method. The 0.10 figure is not a lie, it answers a narrower question. Then the limits
that cut the other way: it is a **median**, **text prompts only**, excludes voice and
images, and **Google does not publish prompt volume**, so no outsider can check any
aggregate built on it.
