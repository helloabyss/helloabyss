## Description

```
Google published the energy cost of a median Gemini Apps text prompt. The paper
contains two numbers, and both are correct:

  0.10 Wh   active TPU and GPU only - the chip doing the inference
  0.17 Wh   plus host machine, idle capacity, data-centre overhead
  0.24 Wh   plus averaging across the whole fleet

That is a 2.4x spread for one prompt. Nothing was concealed - Google printed every
line and the method alongside it. The narrow figure is not dishonest, it answers a
narrower question.

What the number is not: it is a MEDIAN, it covers text prompts only, and it excludes
voice and images. And Google does not publish how many prompts it serves, so nobody
outside the company can turn a per-prompt figure into a credible total.

Based on May 2025 data. Figures are corroborated secondary reporting - the paper and
Google's own blog post were not reachable at the time of writing, so nothing here is
confirmed against the primary document.

When you see one number for anything measured, ask where they drew the line.
```
