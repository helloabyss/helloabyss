Google says one Gemini prompt costs zero point two four watt-hours.

Same paper. Same prompt. Zero point one zero.

Both numbers are correct.

Ten counts the chip doing the thinking.

Seventeen adds the host machine, idle capacity, cooling.

Twenty-four averages it across the whole fleet.

Two point four times apart. One prompt.

Nothing was hidden. Google printed every line.

The number moved because the boundary moved.

Now what it is not.

It is a median. Text only.

No voice. No images.

And Google never publishes how many prompts it serves.

So nobody outside can total it.

One number is never one number.

Ask where they drew the line.
