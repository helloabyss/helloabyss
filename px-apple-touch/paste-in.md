## Title
Apple Put Touch on a MacBook Pro. It Costs $400 Extra.

## Description
```
Apple announced the first touchscreen MacBook Pro at its "Pro and Tell" event on
28 October 2026. The 14-inch starts at $2,899, the 16-inch at $3,399, and both ship
on 6 November.

The number worth knowing is the gap: touch is a $400 premium over the comparable
non-touch M5 model. That comparison is Apple's own.

Sixteen years ago Steve Jobs said touch on a vertical screen doesn't work, and in
2016 Phil Schiller called touch Macs absurd. Apple has now shipped one.

But look at what the $400 actually covers. The panel is a new tandem OLED that Apple
calls PROLED XDR, with the touch sensor built into the panel itself. No source we
could find states whether the cheaper non-touch model carries that same display. If
it doesn't, part of that $400 is the screen, not the touch.

Figures are corroborated secondary reporting (TechCrunch, Quartz, TokenPost, LiveNow
Fox). Apple's own pages were not reachable at the time of writing, so nothing here is
confirmed against a primary release. Sources conflict on the preorder date, so this
video doesn't state one - only the 6 November availability all of them agree on.

Ask whether $400 is the price of touch, or the price of the display.

Drop a comment.
Would you pay $400 to touch your laptop screen?
```
