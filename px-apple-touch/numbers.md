## The chart
The competitor teardown's sharpest finding was "data is charted, not
announced" — their numbers sit in panels with dated axes while this repo was
setting big type. The $400 is now two bars on one fixed scale, so the gap is
something you see. The lower bar is the DERIVED $2,499; it is a bar, never a
spoken or printed figure, and it exists only to make the gap visible.
