## CONFLICT — do not state
**Preorder date.** Quartz and LiveNow Fox say preorders opened 28 Oct; TechCrunch
says 30 Oct. Unresolved. The script says neither; it uses the 6 Nov availability
date, which all sources agree on.

## Grade
Nothing is CONFIRMED. `apple.com` returns HTTP 000 from this container —
verified again today, along with federalreserve.gov, arxiv.org and
en.wikipedia.org. Direct fetch is unavailable; only server-side search works.
The first search returned the $2,899 figure from a **single** outlet; a second
pass corroborated it across three more. That re-check is the whole reason the
number is usable.

## Counter-evidence — must be in the script
1. **Touch is optional.** The non-touch models remain on sale. Nobody is forced
   to pay the premium.
2. **Apple changed its mind in public, and was right to be asked about it.**
   The 2010 and 2016 positions were about a vertical laptop screen; a tandem
   OLED with an in-cell sensor is not the hardware they were describing.
