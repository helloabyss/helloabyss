## 1. Late to Vice Field Guide: Chapters 1–2
`late-to-vice-field-guide-ch1-2.pdf` · 7 pages

**One line:** Stuck and broke in GTA 6's Chapter 1? Settings, free weapons, money and what to do before you get locked into Chapter 2.

**Full:**
The catch-up guide for anyone stuck at the start of GTA 6. It opens with the controller settings
that make aiming and driving feel right. Then it covers two free weapons you can grab early in the
Permit Keys, the Chapter 1 money that actually pays, and the checklist to finish before Tourist
Trap locks you in. The Chapter 2 section shows how to raise the $15,000 you need to leave Port
Gellhorn, the money trap to avoid, mission tips and two collectibles you can miss. Built from
launch-week community guides, with unconfirmed details clearly marked and every source listed.
Unofficial fan guide.

## 2. Late to Vice Road Ahead: Chapters 3–5
`late-to-vice-road-ahead-ch3-5.pdf` · 6 pages

**One line:** What to do before each later chapter of GTA 6 so you don't miss money, missions or collectibles. No spoilers.

**Full:**
A spoiler-free guide to the second half of GTA 6. It covers the rules that apply in every chapter,
the best tips from the biggest launch-week YouTube guides (with the creators credited), and what
you can miss in Chapters 3, 4 and 5. There's a Chapter 3 money table, a mid-game loadout to buy
before Chapter 4, and a checklist for the finale. It also explains the catch with cheats: they only
unlock after the story, and they turn off saving. No plot details, only what you'd want to know
before you get there. Unofficial fan guide.

## 3. Late to Vice Channel Playbook
`late-to-vice-channel-plan.pdf` · 3 pages

**One line:** The plan and weekly routine behind the Late to Vice channel: what to post, when to clip, and how to stay consistent.

**Full:**
The creator playbook for Late to Vice, the GTA 6 channel for players who are still catching up. It
sets out the channel's direction, the three kinds of content it makes, and a simple system for
handling too many ideas. There's a weekly routine built around streaming (about 5–6 hours outside
of streams), a four-beat clip structure for hooks and retention, a pre-posting checklist, the
numbers to check every Sunday, and a plan for the first 7 days. For personal use and for anyone
helping run the channel.
