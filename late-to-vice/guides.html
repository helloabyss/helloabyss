Step 0: fix your settings

Settings › Controls

If missions feel impossible, check your settings before blaming yourself. These are the starting points most guides agree on. Change one thing at a time and play a mission before changing the next.

SettingStart atWhy
Targeting modePartialSnaps to targets but still lets you aim for the head. Go to Lock-On if you keep losing fights, and Free Aim once you're comfortable.
Aim assist strength~4The most common recommendation. Go higher if enemies slip away from your crosshair.
Look sensitivityhigher than aimFast camera to spot enemies, slower aim to land the shot. Guides range from 6 to 9 for look.
Aim sensitivity1–2 below lookIf you overshoot targets, lower this first.
Aim response curveStandardThe safe default. Try Linear or Precision later if aiming feels floaty.
Stick deadzone2–3If the camera drifts on its own, raise it one step at a time until it stops.
Shooting assist / Drive-by assistOnMakes shooting from a car far less painful in chases.
Adaptive trigger resistance (PS5)OffOne guide reports driving feels smoother without the trigger fighting you.
Your capture: settings screen

Aiming habits

  • Keep your crosshair at head height as you move. Less adjusting when you aim in.
  • Tap aim on and off to cycle between targets with Partial targeting.
  • Take cover first, then shoot. Standing in the open is how Chapter 1 shootouts go wrong.
Your capture: a clean chase

Driving habits

  • Brake before the corner, not in it. Then accelerate out.
  • Don't hold full throttle the whole way. Feathering the trigger keeps the car straight.
  • In chases, pick a mid-weight car over the fastest one. It's easier to control.
  • The boat section in Gone Fishing: slow down near the drops. You can't collect packages at full speed.
General tips, not from patch notes

Chapter 1: get out broke-proof

Jason & Lucia · Leonida Keys

How the chapter flows

  1. Prologue

    Meet Lucia at Hamlet Correctional and learn the basic controls.

  2. Gone Fishing

    Follow a plane by boat, collect the packages it drops, then rescue a contact from the Kingfishers.

  3. Brian's errands, any order

    Gear Up, Just a Scratch, Lab Fees and Unwanted Attention unlock together. Do them in whatever order suits you.

  4. Pipework, then Hey Arnold!

    Hey Arnold! gives you a choice. Guides say sparing Arnold pays an extra $10k later, in Chapter 4.

  5. Tourist Trap point of no return

    Starting it locks you into the last three missions with no free roam. Do everything in the checklist below first.

Guides list 22 or 23 missions and disagree on the order. Your map's Story tab is the real list of what's unlocked.

Before you start Tourist Trap

  • Make a separate manual save. Chapter 1 has no mission replay, so an old save is the only fix if something breaks.
  • Move up to $5,000 into your BuckMe account. Guides say it's the only account that carries into Chapter 2.
  • Finish Shirley's Rum and Kenton's treasure dives. Both disappear when Tourist Trap starts.
  • Grab the two free boat weapons below.

Bigger stashes to scout

Tequesta storage unit: grenade launcher, pistol and armor, with an unlocked door and no fight. RV Park bunker, through a trailer near Griffin Shooting Range: H-AT rocket launcher and Type 9 SMG. Guides don't say which chapter these open in.

The Laird 350 sniper has two conflicting locations in guides (a Hamlet motel, or a Kelly County lookout tower). It's left out until someone confirms it.

Small robberies

Businesses pay from a few hundred dollars up to about $1,000 per hit. Have a getaway car parked close by.

Why you're broke (probably)

  • Jason has no ATM access early and carries his cash. Dying or getting busted with a pocket full of money hurts. Stash it at home.
  • Guides say grinding Chapter 1 is barely worth it. Finish the story missions first: they set up better-paying jobs.
  • Aim for $5,000 saved before Tourist Trap. Chapter 2 asks for $15,000 later.

Stuck on a mission?

  • Press down on the D-pad to show the current objective. You may have skipped a step.
  • From the mission panel, try Retry Checkpoint. If it breaks the same way, use Restart Mission.
  • No next mission? Some only unlock after a phone call, a text or some in-game time passing. Do other missions while you wait.

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost

Port Gellhorn · mostly any order

  1. What's in the Bag?

    Opens the chapter.

  2. Get Lucia's vehicle or do Kingpin Bowling Alley first

    Guides recommend one of these before anything else. Most other missions can be done in any order.

  3. Moses, Breakfast Run, Coffee Break

    The big milestones. After Coffee Break, character switching opens the full map for both characters.

  4. Prepare for Vice City needs $15,000

    You can't leave Port Gellhorn without the cash. Start saving from day one of the chapter.

  5. Checking Out

    Closes the chapter.

Guides count 13 or 18 missions. Some only appear after a phone call. If Slam Dunk is missing, keep playing other missions until Dunk calls.

Make the $15,000: sell cars to Moses

Your capture: Moses drop-off
Main Chapter 2 income

How it works

  • About $1,000 or more per car, with a short cooldown between sales.
  • He won't take cars with alarms.
  • Keyless high-end cars can't be opened with the Slim Jim, so skip them.
  • Lucia's first car registration with Moses is free. Later ones cost thousands.
Your capture: Kyle's van
Money trap

Don't pay Kyle $5,000

In Collect Chemicals for Kyle, one guide says paying him $5,000 only returns about $1,000. If you do the mission, take the van by force instead.

Mission tips

Girl's Trip

Pick the calm (Defuse) dialogue option both times to avoid a fight.

Ambulance Chaser

Don't waste ammo on the ambulance. It crashes on its own. Your job is just to keep up.

Slam Dunk

At the docks, reverse out instead of driving forward. Bring healing items.

Breakfast Run

Watch the timer at every stop, and threaten the diner customers or they may attack you.

Missable in Chapter 2

  • Vicki collectible: Starlet Motel office bathroom.
  • Victor Maravilla collectible: on the kitchen fridge.

Both become unreachable after Chapter 2.
