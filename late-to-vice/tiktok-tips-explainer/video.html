Tip 1 · Getaways

Leave the engine running

2× Triangle / Y
Double-tap when you get out. The car stays on for a fast escape.
Read the map
Dark blue buildings can be entered. Pale blue ones are shut.
Tip 2 · Don't lose stuff

Bank it. Stow it.

Wallet cash is lost
Die or get busted and it's gone. Bank as soon as you can.
Guns go in the trunk
Reloading a save erases weapons you didn't stow.
Tip 3 · Get better

Find a shooting range

Ranges build Focus
Focus powers slow-motion aiming. The game never points you there.
Heavy bag at Jason's house
Free stamina training.
Tip 4 · Spend smart

Two buys worth it

A cheap suppressor
Key Lento Ammu-Nation, right after the tutorial.
A garage, when they open
After you leave Key Lento. Free car storage and your loadout.
