### The one factual fix
The original states as fact: *"In 1894, a London newspaper made a prediction…"*

No such article has been found. The quote, the fifty-year figure and the collapsed-conference
detail trace to a single 2004 essay, and The Times refuted the attribution in 2018.

The rebuild keeps the hook and the entire rhetorical payload, but sources it honestly:
*"You've heard this one… No one has ever found that article. But the crisis underneath it
was real."* Everything after that is documented, so the Ford punchline lands **harder**, not
softer — and it inoculates the video against "this is a debunked myth" becoming the top comment.
