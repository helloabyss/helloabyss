# Short — "Retail Sold. The Institutions Bought."

**Status:** scripted, not yet rendered
**Angle:** BofA's weekly equity client flows showed the sixth-largest inflow since 2008 — driven
entirely by institutions and hedge funds, while private clients sold for a sixth straight week.
**Why this one:** The "smart money vs dumb money" frame is the most over-sold narrative in retail
finance content. This short reports the data and then dismantles the conclusion people draw
from it. That is the channel's differentiator.
