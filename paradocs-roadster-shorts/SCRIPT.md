### Short 2 — 2017 NUMBERS STILL ON THE SITE
**On-screen:** `2017 NUMBERS STILL ON THE SITE`
**VO (~20s):**
> Tesla's page still says 1.9 seconds, over 250 miles an hour, 620 miles. Those are 2017 targets. None is an independent production-car test.

Labels: `1.9s / 250+ / 620mi` = CLAIM (2017) · `INDEPENDENT TEST` = NONE

### Short 3 — COLD GAS ≠ TIRE GRIP
**On-screen:** `COLD GAS ≠ TIRE GRIP`
**VO (~22s):**
> The SpaceX package is cold-gas thrusters. They push the chassis, not the rubber. That is how a sub-two-second car even talks about one second — though one-point-one is Musk's claim, not a tested number. Street-legal is a different question.

Labels: `COLD GAS` = REPORTED · `1.1s` = MUSK CLAIM
*(Revision: the "one second" line now attributes the 1.1s figure. It was unattributed in the outline and would have read as a spec.)*

### Short 4 — A71 = RUMOR CODE
**On-screen:** `A71 = RUMOR CODE`
**VO (~22s):**
> Reports call the thruster pack A71 — Falcon-style pressure tanks, maybe ten nozzles, maybe the back seats go away. Until Tesla says it on stage, that is reported, not confirmed.

Labels: `A71` = REPORTED · `10 NOZZLES` = REPORTED · `REAR SEATS` = REPORTED
Hero beat: the whole frame stamps **REPORTED, NOT CONFIRMED** in red.

### Short 5 — TWO CARS, ONE HEADLINE
**On-screen:** `TWO CARS, ONE HEADLINE`
**VO (~24s):**
> Watch for two products: a normal electric Roadster, and a limited thruster car that may never be street legal. If they only demo the flyer, that is not the car most reservation holders get.
