## VO SCRIPT (148 words)

**[0:00–0:08] HOOK**
> Almost everybody gets Moore's Law wrong — including people who quote it for a living.

**[0:08–0:24] THE CORRECTION**
> It was never a prediction that chips get faster. In April nineteen sixty-five, Gordon Moore
> published an article in Electronics magazine. Read his own subtitle: with unit cost falling
> as the number of components rises. What he plotted was the number of components you could fit
> on a chip **at minimum cost per component**.

**[0:24–0:34] THE PAYOFF**
> Moore's Law is economics. Not physics. And it carried two separate promises: more transistors,
> and **cheaper** transistors.

**[0:34–0:44] THE TURN**
> The first one still holds. The second one has stopped. A two-nanometre wafer now costs more
> than the three-nanometre wafer it replaces — roughly ten to twenty percent more.

**[0:44–0:50] CLOSE**
> Fifty years of automatic discount, expired. Watch the cost, not the chip. This is Paradocs 10X.
